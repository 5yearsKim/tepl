# Research

Research papers, mathematical arguments, proposals, and finite validation
experiments live here. These documents are separate from the
[current implementation design](../design/README.md) and
[measured compiler experiments](../labs/README.md).

- [Attribute synthesis](attribute-synthesis/README.md): paper, completeness and
  soundness arguments, independent drafts, supporting scripts, and archived work.

Each collection identifies its primary documents, admitted domains, and status.
A proof draft describes its own mathematical procedure; it does not establish
that production TEPL implements that procedure completely. Finite checks provide
supporting evidence rather than machine-checked proof.

Keep authored drafts independent until their arguments are reconciled. Archive
superseded approaches with context and links to their successors. Preserve
mathematical text when reorganizing files; update links and command paths.
